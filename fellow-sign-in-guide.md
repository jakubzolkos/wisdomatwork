## Part 1: One-page guide for Fellows

### What you need each time you sign in
- **Your program email address**, the one the Wisdom at Work team has on file for you.
- **Access to that inbox.** We email you a 6-digit code each time.

That's all. You don't need an app or a password.

### Signing in
1. Go to the portal and open the **Sign in** page.
2. Type your program email.
3. Choose **Email me a code**.
4. Open your email. You'll get a message from "WaW Fellows Portal" with a 6-digit code. It works once and expires after 10 minutes. If you don't see it, check your spam folder, or ask for a new code.
5. Type the code into the portal. You'll land on your dashboard.

### Prefer a password?
On the Sign in page choose **Set or reset your password**. We'll email you a link (it works for 24 hours). Once you've chosen a password you can sign in with your email and password instead of a code.

### Once you're in
- **The menu on the left** is your program: your sessions, readings and surveys, in order.
- **Your dashboard** shows what to do next and when your next session is.
- **Your Unique ID** is on your dashboard and your profile. Every survey asks for it. When you open a survey from the portal, it copies your ID for you to paste where the form asks.
- **About** shows your **Key Dates**: your live sessions, with times in Eastern.
- **Library** holds recommended reading and listening.
- **Your profile** has your own documents, such as your personal Orientation Guide.
- **Joining a live session:** open the session in the left menu on the day. The Join button appears there. The team also emails you the morning of each session, with a Join on Zoom button and a calendar invite you can add to your calendar.

### If something goes wrong
- **"This link has expired":** links from emails work for 24 hours and only once. On the page that says so, type your email and we'll send a new one.
- **No code in your inbox:** wait a minute, check spam, then ask for a new code.
- **Still stuck:** write to waw@abigailadamsinstitute.org.
